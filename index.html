About Us

Focused on biologics, discovery, and intelligent design.

Sapia Bio is being built around three complementary priorities: the development of a portfolio of antibody candidates against diverse targets, the creation of partner-ready discovery capabilities, and the application of advanced computational methods to accelerate antibody design.

Our intent is to build practical, high-value capabilities that support both internal program advancement and external collaboration across the antibody discovery ecosystem.

Services

Antibody discovery and design capabilities built for collaboration.

Biologics Development

We are advancing a portfolio of antibody candidates across a range of targets, with a small number of lead programs representing a core strategic focus.

Discovery Partnerships

Sapia is positioned to support external partners through antibody discovery programs and enabling access to high-quality candidate generation capabilities.

AI-Assisted Design

Program Enablement

Our work is designed to accelerate discovery and decision-making through robust scientific planning, iterative experimentation, and practical execution support.

Team

Your scientific collaborators, built around outcomes.

Dr. Ivelin Georgiev

Chief Scientific Officer

Leads translational strategy and guides the scientific design of partner programs with a strong focus on feasibility and clarity. Dr. Georgiev is Director of the Vanderbilt Center for Computational Microbiology and Immunology and Professor of Pathology, Microbiology & Immunology and Computer Science at Vanderbilt. His research focuses on antibody discovery and immunotherapy development. He is the inventor of multiple issued patents, including antigen IP underlying an U.S. Food and Drug Administration-approved vaccine, and has extensive experience translating computational and immunological discoveries into therapeutic applications.

Dr. Perry Wasdin

Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Wasdin is a Data Scientist at Vanderbilt University Medical Center. He is an inventor of the MAGE technology for AI antibody design, with expertise in machine learning and B cell immunology. He brings prior experience in bioinformatics data engineering and biotech startup assessment.

Dr. Sabina Leonard

Chief Technical Officer

Sabina Leonard recently completed her Ph.D., bringing nascent expertise in antibody and nanobody discovery. She has led projects resulting in multiple peer-reviewed publications and provisional patents. Previously, she served as an Assistant Scientist at Janssen Pharmaceutical Companies of Johnson & Johnson.
